Assignment sets (24)

Each participant sees 6 of the 36 combinations: every case once, every modality twice (once correct, once flawed), and one error on an easy, a medium and a hard question. There are 24 such sets, and new participants get the set with the fewest finished + in-progress surveys. Each block of 24 participants therefore fills every one of the 36 combinations exactly 4 times. Hover a tile to see what's in it.
