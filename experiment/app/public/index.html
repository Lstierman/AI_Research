AI Research Seminar · Ghent University · Demo

Do we believe an AI's explanation more when it looks human?

An AI explains six unfamiliar topics, from poker to fraud statistics, and participants answer three questions about each. Half of the explanations contain one subtle flaw. Each part of the study uses a different modality, from text to voice to video, and we measure who picks up the flaw.
