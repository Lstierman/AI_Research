## Pitch 1

### 2. Title

**Better models, better humans? Human-AI interaction from chatbots to face-to-face AI**

### 3. Main takeaways

A better AI model doesn't automatically make the human using it better: on average, human-AI teams perform worse than the best of human or AI alone, and people overestimate how accurate LLM answers are. Interaction is moving from text to voice and real-time video (potentially BCI in the future), so the AI feels more human, and questions of trust and overreliance become more pressing.

### 4. Constructive feedback

**Strengths**
- Very high practical relevance
- Accessible, engaging topic with strong demos

**Weaknesses**
- A proper experiment needs human participants
- The field is broad
- Some key evidence (Tavus, OpenAI, METR) is not yet peer-reviewed

## Pitch 2

### 5. Title

**Models that keep learning: continual learning beyond frozen LLMs**

### 6. Main takeaways

Today's LLMs are frozen after training. Updating them on new data causes catastrophic forgetting and gradually destroys their ability to learn at all (Nature 2024). Recent methods such as sparse memory fine-tuning and self-adapting models (SEAL) point towards models that keep learning after deployment, the lack of this ability is widely seen as a key bottleneck for AI.

### 7. Constructive feedback

**Strengths**
- Clear, well-defined scientific problem with strong recent results
- Very suitable for a reproducibility study
- High practical relevance

**Weaknesses**
- Several of the most exciting LLM-scale methods (Titans, Nested Learning, sparse memory FT) have no official code
- Small-scale results (MNIST, CIFAR) may not transfer to LLMs
- More technical topic
