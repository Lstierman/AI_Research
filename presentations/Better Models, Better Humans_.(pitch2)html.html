Why this matters

AI is climbing a ladder of human-likeness

Text

Read at your own pace. Easy to re-check a sentence.

Voice

Feels like a person talking. Passes once, harder to re-check.

Face-to-face

Eye contact and expression add social cues. Oct 2026: 26 of 54 people on a one-minute video call thought an AI avatar was human.

Each step feels more natural. Does it also make people accept errors more easily?

Tavus Research, “Griffin”, 1 Oct 2026 (self-reported)
What we already know

Explanations persuade. Do a voice and a face add to it?

Explanations raise acceptance and confidence, also when the AI is wrong.Bansal et al., CHI 2021 · Steyvers et al., Nature Mach. Intell. 2025
The same words sound more competent when heard than when read.Schroeder & Epley, Psych. Science 2015
Video is believed more than text, but persuades only slightly more.Sundar et al., JCMC 2021 · Wittenberg et al., PNAS 2021
AI avatars can lower trust, while lie-detection accuracy stays the same.Fernández, Hancock & Jakesch, CHI 2026
The gap: no study we found gives the same AI explanation, with one planted mistake, as text, voice and face, and measures whether people follow it.
Research question

When people learn from an AI, does a more human-like format make them adopt its mistakes?

Trust

Voice and face are trusted more

Rated right after each explanation, before answering.

Main hypothesis · flaw adoption

More human-like, more mistakes followed

With voice, and even more with a face, more people give the wrong answer the flaw leads to.

Difficulty

Bigger effect on harder questions

Checking costs more effort there, so social cues matter more.

Elaboration likelihood model, Petty & Cacioppo 1986

Also measured: does confidence match correctness, do people say they checked, which format do they prefer. All tests are two-sided.

What we measure

Do people follow the mistake, and do they know it?

Flaw adoption · main outcomeOn the affected question, did they give exactly the wrong answer the flaw leads to?
Learning from correct explanationsIs a format simply harder to learn from? That's a different story from more convincing.
TrustRated right after each explanation, before answering.
CalibrationDoes confidence match whether the answer is actually correct?

Analysis: mixed logistic regression with participant and case as random effects, compared across text, voice and voice + face.

Is this feasible?

Small, online, and honest about its limits

What it takes

  • 36 participants minimum (one per condition), ideally 72
  • A 30-minute online survey on a laptop, collected within one week
  • The survey site already works: demo on the next slide
  • Still to record: 12 scripts × 3 formats = 36 clips (TTS voice + avatar tool)

What it can't do

  • Detect only large effects: we present it as an exploratory pilot
  • Text can be re-read, voice can't: voice vs. face is the cleanest comparison
  • One voice and one avatar, so results may depend on them
  • Mild deception: everyone gets a full debrief that corrects every mistake
