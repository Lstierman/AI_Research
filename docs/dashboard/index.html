3 · Certainty & calibration

Certainty 1 (not sure at all) – 5 (completely sure). Confident adoption of an error is the overreliance signal.

Mean certainty by form and outcome

Outcome on error-targeted questions only: resisted = correct answer, adopted = the error's answer, other = another wrong answer. "All correct-version questions" is the reference.

Calibration by form (all questions)

Certainty rescaled to 0–1 ((c−1)/4). Overconfidence = mean certainty − accuracy (positive = more sure than right). Brier = mean (certainty − correct)²; lower is better. Gap = certainty when correct − when wrong; bigger = people know when they're right.
